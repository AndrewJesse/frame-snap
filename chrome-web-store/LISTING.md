Paste these into the store listing form. The summary is also the `description` field in `manifest.json`, so “Summary from package” matches after you upload `chrome-web-store/framesnap-1.0.0.zip`.

## Title

FrameSnap

## Summary

Capture video frames on your device. Frames never leave this browser. https://free-utils.com

## Description

FrameSnap captures the current video frame on your device and keeps the snapshot in this browser. Frames never leave this browser.

Open the popup and choose Capture Current Frame. Download the image, copy it, or delete it, and click a timestamp to return the video to that moment.
